# Eveline: support

Something not working, a question, or an idea? Open an issue here:

**https://github.com/Winds7799/eveline-updates/issues**

Tell us your device and what happened, and we will get back to you.

## Common questions

**How do I get updates?** Fixes and updates arrive by themselves: the game
downloads them in the background when it starts, and uses them the next
time you open it. The title screen says "Update ready: restart Eveline"
when one is waiting.

**I can't hear Halloran coming.** Play with headphones if you can: the
game is built around sound. Check the volume in Settings.

**How do I skip a cinematic?** Hold a finger on the screen.

**Where is Part 2?** Escape the night ward through the main gate. Once you
have reached it, the title screen shows Continue · Part 2.
